Stranded on Lingas

Unity 2D | C# | Team of 4

Created for Ludum Dare 59 - Theme: Signal. Programmed dialogue system, radio interface, radio call system, monster appearance mechanic.

  • Made in 72 hours from scratch.
  • Fully custom Dialogue module, with story flags to track progression, and sound effects tied to lines of dialogue.
  • Radio interface tied in with Dialogue module, activating dialogue based on story flags and radio settings.

Ashes of Asterra

Unity 2D | C# | Group of 8

Created for Year 1 of GAME: Design and Simulation @ Georgian College. Built a 3-level 2D action platformer, focused on exploration and story.

  • Created each Enemy AI, including the Final Boss.
